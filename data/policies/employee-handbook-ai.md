# Northwoods Employee Handbook: AI

## 1. New tool approval
Before trying a new AI tool for firm work, send the AI owner the tool name, intended use, and information involved. Wait for written approval and use the approved firm account.

## 2. Tool inventory
Record AI tools already used for work in the firm's AI tool inventory, including the tool name, owner, purpose, and approval status. Send the information to the AI owner if you cannot access the inventory.

## 3. Incident reporting
If you have already entered client data into an unapproved tool or suspect a disclosure, stop using the tool and promptly tell the AI owner or your manager. Record what was entered, the tool, and when it happened; do not delete evidence or attempt to conceal the incident. The AI owner coordinates the response and any required notifications.

## 4. Review and escalation
A person must review AI-generated work before sharing it externally. Ask your manager or the AI owner when a policy is unclear; the assistant does not grant exceptions.
