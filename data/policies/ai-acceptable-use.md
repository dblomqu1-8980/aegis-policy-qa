# Northwoods AI Acceptable-Use Policy

## 1. Scope and approved tools
Northwoods Professional Services is a fictional firm. For firm work, employees may use only AI tools and firm-managed accounts approved by the AI owner. Do not use a free personal ChatGPT account for firm work. Request approval before trying a new tool.

## 2. Confidential and sensitive information
Do not enter client confidential information, client records, Social Security numbers, passwords, or other sensitive information into unapproved AI tools. An approved exception requires written authorization from the AI owner and the client when required. Approval for a tool does not automatically authorize every type of data.

## 3. Human review
AI may help draft a client deliverable, but a qualified employee must review its accuracy, confidentiality, and appropriateness before it is shared. AI output is not a substitute for professional judgment.

## 4. Vendor data use
Before approving an AI vendor, the AI owner reviews whether customer inputs or outputs are used to train models, along with retention, security, and contract terms. Do not assume a vendor's training settings are suitable without that review.
