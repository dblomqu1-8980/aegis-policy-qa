# NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 excerpts

## GOVERN — oversight and accountability (page 22)
GOVERN is a cross-cutting function that is infused throughout AI risk management and enables the other functions of the process. Documentation can enhance transparency, improve human review processes, and bolster accountability in AI system teams.

## MEASURE — assessing risk (page 20, Figure 5)
Identified risks are assessed, analyzed, or tracked.
